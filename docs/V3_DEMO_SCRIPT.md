# V3 hackathon demonstration — about six minutes

## Prepare

Use V3b first: `VOICE_PROVIDER=deterministic`, `JOB_SOURCE=demo`, a fresh isolated database and `seed-demo-v3`. Choose the demonstration password privately. Have two browser profiles ready: a fresh worker registration and `employer1.v3@example.invalid`. Keep the existing seeded worker as a recovery account. Use actual demonstration hardware's audio preflight before presenting; keep typing available.

Say: “ShramLink helps industrial workers explain their skills in Hindi and understand opportunities before applying. This is a demonstration. External applications are never sent.”

## 0:00–0:45 — The redesigned entry

Show the large Worker and Employer entry cards. Point to **बोलें → काम देखें → आवेदन करें**. Open worker registration and use a synthetic demonstration phone/password. Explain that these credentials are local demo accounts, not an SMS-verified identity.

## 0:45–2:15 — One conversation

Press **बातचीत शुरू करें** once. Listen to “नमस्ते। आपका नाम क्या है?” and confirm the audio with “जी हाँ”. Then answer:

1. “मेरा नाम रमेश कुमार है।”
2. “अट्ठाईस साल।”
3. “मुझे वेल्डिंग और हेल्पर का काम आता है।”
4. “कारखाना।”
5. “दो साल आठ महीने।”
6. “दिल्ली।”
7. “मेरे पास ई श्रम नंबर नहीं है।”

During review say “मेरा नाम बदलो”, then “रमेश शर्मा”. Hear the corrected review. Show that experience retains **32 months** while the summary displays **2 completed years**. Say “हाँ” or “सेव कर दो” only after checking. Reload the dashboard to show persistence.

If the device has no suitable Hindi voice or microphone access fails, press **लिखें**, complete the same fields, tick the review confirmation and save. Describe this as the working alternative; do not pretend a typed session is a successful real voice test.

## 2:15–3:00 — Hear and practise discovery

Press **काम खोजें**. In the first-entry tutorial, press **निर्देश सुनें**. Practise up, down, left and right on the sample card; it cannot create an application. Press Replay to hear the current instruction again, Stop to silence it, or **अभी काम देखें** to skip. Help reopens it.

Use **काम सुनें** on the real fictional vacancy. Point to wage unit, city, shift, required months, employer and explicit informal/formal category. These are self-reported demonstration terms, not verified employment promises.

## 3:00–4:15 — Direct internal recruitment

Choose the Delhi daytime welder job owned by employer 1. Swipe left once and undo it. Swipe right or press Apply: show that a confirmation opens before any submission. Confirm. Reload **मेरे आवेदन**.

In the second browser, employer 1 reviews the applicant's self-reported skills and exact months, then selects the worker. Reload the worker dashboard and show the matching Selected status. Explain the vacancy-capacity and duplicate checks. This is an isolated demo workflow, not an actual hiring outcome.

## 4:15–5:15 — Formal pathway, honest external simulation

In discovery choose a fictional external opportunity (filter skill to welder and city to Noida if useful). Show **₹26,000 / महीना**, its external source/category and the persistent local-only notice. Open **प्रदर्शन तैयारी**.

Read the source link, actual saved qualifications and missing documents. Confirm the local preparation. The dashboard must say:

**Demo application prepared — not submitted externally**

Repeat: “यह प्रदर्शन है। बाहरी कंपनी को कोई आवेदन नहीं भेजा गया है।” Do not call this Applied, Submitted, Accepted or Hired. Do not open the fictional `example.invalid` URL expecting a real employer page.

## 5:15–6:00 — Configuration and limits

Show the mode banner only from a seeded demonstration account. Explain that one codebase supports optional xAI interpretation and a public Greenhouse feed. The default works without either service.

Be precise: four configurations were exercised with simulated provider responses; no authenticated xAI call was made here. A live public feed fetch succeeded but yielded no supported NCR industrial opportunities, so the demo uses labelled fictional jobs. Physical speech compatibility and public HTTPS deployment still need validation on the chosen device/host.

Do not advertise real third-party applications, government verification, universal voice compatibility, advanced AI matching, escrow or automatic payments. Leave the pitch deck unchanged until the team chooses verified claims.
