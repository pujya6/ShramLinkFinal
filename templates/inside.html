01 · THE IMPLEMENTED PATH

How an answer becomes an opportunity

The arrows describe implemented components. Reading this page starts no microphone, provider request or database mutation.

  1. 1

    Speech or typing

    Browser Hindi recognition, selected Hindi synthesis and a full typed alternative.

    Bounded turn manager ↗→
  2. 2

    Interpretation

    Local Hindi/Hinglish parser first. Optional xAI handles unresolved short answers; errors return to local clarification.

    Bounded optional adapter ↗→
  3. 3

    Validate & confirm

    Structured fields, age/experience rules, corrections and explicit review. Filling every field does not save.

    Server validation ↗↓
  4. 4

    Saved worker profile

    SQLAlchemy persists self-reported skills, city and exact experience months. No government-verification claim.

    Profile models ↗→
  5. 5

    Explainable matching

    Skill, city, domain and experience rank direct jobs. Skill and exact minimum months determine application eligibility.

    Matching and capacity rules ↗→
  6. 6

    One job at a time

    Hear the actual vacancy. Swipe or use buttons. Skip, undo and review before applying. External cards keep their source and missing information.

    Discovery controller ↗↓
  7. Direct · internal

    Application → employer decision

    Confirmed internal application, duplicate prevention, authorized employer selection and shared status. Capacity rules remain enforced.

    External · demonstration only

    Review → local preparation

    Signed confirmation of the saved profile and source vacancy. Separate local record; no third-party application sender.

    Demo application prepared — not submitted externally
